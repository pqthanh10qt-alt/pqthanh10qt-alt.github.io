Tigo Social Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Tigo Social Manager ("the App"), operated by HO KINH DOANH TAM LINH VIET ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Information we receive from TikTok and Facebook

When an authorized user connects an account, we receive only what the user approves:

  • TikTok: an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID, and basic profile info (display name / username) under the user.info.basic scope; creator posting settings needed to publish under the video.publish scope.
  • Facebook: Page access tokens, Page ID and Page name for Pages the user manages and selects.

We do not receive or store passwords.

2. How we use this information

  • To publish videos and posts that the user prepares in the App to the user's own connected accounts.
  • To show which account is connected and whether a post was published successfully.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Where information is stored

All data (connection tokens, post drafts, schedules, media files and logs) is stored locally on the computer where the App is installed. We do not run a server that collects this data. Videos and images are sent directly from that computer to TikTok or Facebook through their official APIs when the user publishes.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with any third party. Content is only sent to the platform the user chooses to publish to.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on the user's computer until the user deletes it. A user can at any time:

  • Disconnect an account in the App (Channels > Disconnect) and delete posts or media in the App.
  • Revoke the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) or in Facebook (Settings > Business integrations).
  • Uninstall the App and delete its data folder.

6. Security

Tokens are stored only on the local computer, are never shown in full in the App's interface, and are transmitted only over encrypted HTTPS connections to TikTok and Meta.

7. Children

The App is an internal business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version and its effective date will always be published on this page.
