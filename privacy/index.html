Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TIGO Social Manager (“the Service”), developed under the TigoSoft software brand and operated by HỘ KINH DOANH TÂM LINH VIỆT in Vietnam (“TigoSoft”, “we”, “us”), handles information when users install the application, connect third-party accounts, or use supported online services.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features a user chooses and the permissions granted by a connected platform, we may process:

  • Basic account information such as platform user ID/open ID, display name, username and avatar.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Content selected by the user for publishing, including media files, captions, publishing settings and scheduling information.
  • Operational information such as publishing status, error logs and connection status used to provide and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Customer interaction data only when the connected platform provides authorized API access and the user enables the related feature.

We do not ask users to provide their TikTok, Facebook or other social platform passwords to TIGO Social Manager.

2. How we use information

  • To connect accounts that the user is authorized to manage.
  • To display the connected account and available platform settings.
  • To upload or publish content only after the user selects or schedules the action.
  • To synchronize supported interactions and provide customer-management features when permitted by the platform.
  • To maintain security, diagnose errors and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal information or use connected-account data to build advertising profiles.

3. TikTok data

When a user connects TikTok, authorization is performed through TikTok's own authorization interface. TIGO Social Manager requests only the scopes required for the enabled feature. TikTok API functionality is used only when the application has the required TikTok approval and the user has granted the corresponding permission.

4. Storage and transmission

TIGO Social Manager is primarily a desktop application. Drafts and local media remain on the user's device unless the user chooses a feature that uploads or publishes them. Platform tokens and integration metadata are stored only as required to provide connected-account functions and may be held in secure local storage or TigoSoft server-side components when an integration requires server-side OAuth exchange, token refresh, callbacks, scheduling or API processing. Data sent between the Service and supported platforms is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Sharing

We share information only as necessary to provide a user-requested feature, such as sending selected content to the social platform chosen by the user or using infrastructure providers needed to operate the Service. We do not sell or rent connected-account information.

6. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or until the user disconnects the account or requests deletion, subject to any retention required by law or the connected platform.

7. User choices and deletion

Users may disconnect a connected account inside TIGO Social Manager and may revoke the application's access in the third-party platform's own settings. Instructions for deleting local application data and requesting deletion of server-side data are available on our Data Deletion page.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect credentials and application data. Tokens are not displayed in full in normal application screens. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their computer and platform accounts.

9. Children

TIGO Social Manager is intended for business users, creators and authorized content teams and is not directed to children under 13. Where a higher minimum age applies under local law or a connected platform's terms, that higher age applies.

10. Third-party platforms

TikTok, Meta and other connected services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of information is governed by those policies.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, applicable law or platform requirements change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.
