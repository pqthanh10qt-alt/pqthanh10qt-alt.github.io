Support

TIGO Social Manager

Account connection

Use the “Connect” action inside TIGO Social Manager. For supported platforms, authentication takes place on the platform's own authorization screen. TigoSoft does not ask for your social platform password.

Publishing issues

Confirm that the connected account still has valid authorization, that the requested permission is available, and that the selected media complies with the destination platform's format and policy requirements.

Disconnect an account

Use the disconnect option in TIGO Social Manager and, when needed, revoke the application's authorization from the connected platform's own settings.

Privacy or deletion request

See our Privacy Policy and Data Deletion instructions.
